Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what 1379 Holdings LLC collects when you use LeadHound and what we do with it.

What we collect from you

When you sign up, we collect your name, business name, email address, phone number (if you give it), and the customer profile you fill out in our onboarding form (industries, service area, preferences). Stripe collects your payment details to process billing; we receive only limited information from Stripe, such as your name, email, plan, and the last 4 digits of your card. Our website uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that counts visits without cookies or tracking you across sites.

How we use it

We use your information to build and deliver your lead lists, manage your subscription and billing, answer your questions, and send you service messages (such as receipts and delivery notices). We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share only what's needed with the services that help us run LeadHound: Stripe (payments), FormSubmit (receives our website forms), our email provider (sends your lists), and Google (if you choose shared-spreadsheet delivery). We may also share information if the law requires it.

Business data in our lists

Our lead lists contain business contact information gathered from public and openly licensed sources such as OpenStreetMap. If you own a business in our data and want it removed from future lists, contact us and we'll exclude it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your subscription is active and for up to 2 years afterward for tax and billing records, unless you ask us to delete it sooner and we're not required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by contacting us. You can unsubscribe from non-essential emails using the link in any email.

Children

LeadHound is a business service and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let active customers know by email.
